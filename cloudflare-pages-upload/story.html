Our Story

A response rooted in compassion.

The story of Sakinah holds grief with care—and carries forward the compassion, solidarity and connection shown by communities after 15 March 2019.

سكينةSakinahTranquillity · reassurance · peace

A feeling of being at peace, settled and belonging.

The name Sakinah is inspired by verses in the Qur’an. It describes tranquillity, calmness, reassurance and peace. Sakinah Community Trust exists to support initiatives that help restore and grow that feeling—in the lives of survivors and across the wider community.

We believe Muslim women can be active agents of change. The Trust is women-led and includes family members of those lost in the attacks, with a commitment to honouring their legacy through long-term community response and engagement.

Some donations received by the Christchurch Foundation were entrusted to Sakinah. The Trust sees this as an amānah—a responsibility to steward those gifts so their generosity can continue to help communities connect, grow and thrive.

A continuing response

Remembering with purpose.

15 MAR2019

A day that changed Christchurch

A terrorist attack at two Christchurch mosques killed 51 people and injured many others. In the days that followed, people across Aotearoa responded with compassion and solidarity. Sakinah carries that spirit forward through community-led support.

COMMUNITYRESPONSE

Turning goodwill toward community care

Donations given to the Muslim community after the tragedy were managed by the Christchurch Foundation. Sakinah Community Trust now manages grants that direct that legacy toward community-led initiatives.

WORKONGOING

Making room for belonging

Through its purpose and funding work, Sakinah supports connection, social inclusion, wellbeing and opportunities for communities to take part.
