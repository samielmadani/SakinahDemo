Funding • Apply Now

Funding for community projects.

Sakinah supports community-led projects in Christchurch. Start by checking who can apply and whether your idea fits the Trust’s stated purposes.

Grant options

Three ways to support a project.

The Board confirms the grant type and any conditions if an application is successful.

Full funding

May cover the total cost of a small project, up to NZ$2,000.

Partial funding

May contribute part of a proposed project’s cost.

Grant matching

May require the applicant to raise an agreed share. The Trust confirms the match if approved.

Who can apply?

Christchurch-based organisations—not individuals.

Eligible applicants include groups, incorporated societies, charitable trusts and limited liability companies registered for charitable purposes.

If your grant is approved

Spend funds within three calendar months. If the project does not go ahead, the Trust may request the grant back. A brief report and event photos are requested.

Questions, before you start

Good to know.

Can I apply as an individual?

No. Funding is available to Christchurch-based groups, incorporated societies, charitable trusts and limited liability companies registered for charitable purposes. Individuals cannot apply.

What is the maximum grant?

Full funding may cover the total cost of a small project up to NZ$2,000. Partial and matched grants are also available; the amount depends on the project and the Trust’s decision.

When can I apply?

Contact the Trust to ask about application timing and any deadlines.

Where is the application form?

Contact the Trust to request an application form.

How long do I have to spend the grant?

Funds must be spent within three calendar months of receipt. The Trust may request the grant back if the project does not proceed.

What reporting is required?

Grant recipients are asked to provide a brief report explaining how the funds were spent, what worked well, what could be improved and photos of events.

Ready to ask about funding?

Contact the Trust to request an application form.
