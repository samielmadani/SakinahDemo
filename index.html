سكينة Sakinah Tranquillity · reassurance · peace

A feeling of calm, safety and belonging.

Sakinah Community Trust is a women-led Christchurch organisation. Our name speaks to a state of tranquillity, reassurance and peace—and to the community connections that help it grow.

We carry forward the compassion shown after 15 March 2019 by supporting initiatives that bring people together and strengthen the wellbeing of our communities.
